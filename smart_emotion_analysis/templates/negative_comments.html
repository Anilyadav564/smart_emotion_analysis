💡 How AI Classifies Negative Feedback

Not all negative comments are bad. Many contain your video's most valuable optimization signals. Our pipeline separates comments into three distinct tiers:

1. Constructive Criticism
Points out pacing, audio levels, or unclear steps with actionable context. High creator value.
2. Critical Complaints
Expresses general disappointment or confusion without specific solutions.
3. Toxic / Abusive Content
Contains insults, harassment, profanity, or threats. Zero instructional value.
